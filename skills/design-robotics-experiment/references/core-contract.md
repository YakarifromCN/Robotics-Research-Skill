# 实验核心合同

输入只引用 Research Card 的稳定 ID 与 claim digest。实验义务必须落到条件、对比、指标、实验单位和判定规则。结果工件引用 design digest，验证器从结构化估计机械重算结论。失败、abort、安全事件和偏离均为数据。

---

# English

Reference the Research Card by stable ID and claim digest. Map obligations to conditions, contrasts, metrics, units, and rules. Bind results to the design digest and recompute verdicts mechanically. Failures, aborts, safety events, and deviations remain data.
