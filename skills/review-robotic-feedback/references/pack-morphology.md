# 形态与仿生包 / Morphology and bio-inspired pack

检查形态变量是否承重、替代形态是否公平、结构制造差异、控制器配平和机制消融。不要把形态演示直接等同于生物学解释。

Check whether morphology variables are load-bearing, whether alternative bodies are fair, and whether fabrication, controller retuning, and mechanism ablations are separated. Do not equate a morphology demo with a biological explanation.
