# 软体机器人包 / Soft-robotics pack

检查材料批次、形变测量、迟滞、接触与边界条件、制造重复性、寿命和安全。不要把单个柔性样机行为外推为材料普遍规律。

Check material batch, deformation measurements, hysteresis, contact and boundary conditions, fabrication repeatability, lifetime, and safety. Do not generalize one soft prototype to a universal material law.
