# 临床与辅助机器人包 / Clinical and assistive robotics pack

检查受试者纳入、伦理、风险、训练和随访、个体差异、临床终点与工程代理指标的边界。工程结果不能静默升级为疗效结论。

Check inclusion, ethics, risk, training and follow-up, individual variation, clinical endpoints, and the boundary between engineering proxies and clinical efficacy. Engineering results must not silently become treatment claims.
