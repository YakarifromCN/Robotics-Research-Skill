# Writing 阶段的子流形路由

Claim Ledger 的每个承重主张应绑定一个或多个 E/P/C/L/D/H/A/S 轴，并绑定相应结果、数字、引文、图表和证据状态。轴向量用于检查叙事高度是否超过证据形状、将指定 venue 的官方 scope 转成组织/压缩/补充材料要求，并明确未覆盖的轴向负担，输出 `EVIDENCE_GAPS` 或收窄 claim boundary。

factor-fit ranking 只能帮助选择候选载体，不能在正文中写成录用概率、领域等级或“适合所以有效”。ratings 不进入 Claim Ledger。

---

# English

Bind every load-bearing Claim Ledger statement to one or more E/P/C/L/D/H/A/S axes and to its results, numbers, citations, figures, and evidence state. Use the vector to check whether narrative altitude exceeds evidence, turn the target venue's current official scope into organization/compression/supplement requirements, and expose uncovered axis burden as `EVIDENCE_GAPS` or a narrowed claim boundary.

Factor-fit ranking may help choose a venue, but it is not an acceptance probability, field rank, or evidence of effectiveness. Ratings never enter the Claim Ledger.
