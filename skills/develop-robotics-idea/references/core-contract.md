# 核心科学合同

科学对象只有三层：Claim Shape 描述主张形状；Evidence Obligations 逐项说明主张需要的证据；Domain Packs 只在相关时加载。目标载体、格式和读者适配保存在独立快照中。

锁分三类：Claim Lock 只能回到 Idea 改；Design Lock 在观察结果前冻结；Operational Mutable 可在保留版本和理由后细化。证据画像按分量比较，不把仿真、实机、现场、时长、独立性压成单一序列。

停止状态是有效产物。不得把缺数据、负结果或不确定结果转述为成功。

---

# English

Use only three layers: Claim Shape, explicit Evidence Obligations, and conditionally loaded Domain Packs. Keep target and formatting adaptation in a separate snapshot. Separate Claim Lock, Design Lock, and versioned operational refinements. Compare evidence component by component and preserve stopping states.
